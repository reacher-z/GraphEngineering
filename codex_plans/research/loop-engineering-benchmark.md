# Loop Engineering benchmark snapshot

Snapshot date: 2026-07-26

Source: <https://github.com/cobusgreyling/loop-engineering>

## Public benchmark

- 9,416 stars, 1,290 forks, and 60 watchers at the final launch-readiness rescan.
- Created 2026-06-09 and reached about 5.5k stars by 2026-07-04.
- Roughly 599 tracked files and about 40k lines across core text/code formats.
- Thirteen package manifests under its tool suite and nineteen GitHub Actions workflows.
- Large content surface: patterns, starters, examples, docs, stories, templates, safety material, and contribution automation.

## Product strengths to match

- A memorable concept with a short, repeatable explanation.
- One CLI front door, five-minute onboarding, doctor, readiness score, and badge.
- Tool-aware starters for several agent harnesses.
- Safety, failure, budget, state, worktree, and operating guidance.
- Interactive showcase, launch content, social assets, good-first-issue inventory, and contributor recognition.

## Technical opportunity to exceed

Loop Engineering primarily packages a methodology and toolchain and directs
users to companion projects for a general runtime. Graph Engineering will ship
the runtime itself, with:

- Versioned, type-checked edge contracts.
- Native TypeScript and Python execution.
- Durable node-level writes, resume, replay, and fork.
- Pipelines, barriers, dynamic revisions, and bounded cycles with precise semantics.
- Verifier gates and structured unknown outcomes.
- Runtime topology, critical-path, and recovery visualization.

## Metric interpretation

Stars indicate broad awareness but are not equivalent to retained users. Graph
Engineering therefore tracks successful graph runs, seven-day retained
repositories, external adopters, outside contributors, and first-success time
alongside the 6,000-star launch target.
