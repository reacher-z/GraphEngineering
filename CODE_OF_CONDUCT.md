# Community code of conduct

Graph Engineering is committed to a respectful, inclusive, technically honest
community. Be constructive, assume good intent while evaluating evidence, give
credit, respect privacy, and make space for people with different experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate misinformation,
credential exposure, spam, and manipulation of project metrics are not
acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove violating content and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security/contact channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical, and retaliation against reporters is prohibited.
